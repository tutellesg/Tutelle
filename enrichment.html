Frequently asked questions

Is there a fee for parents?

No. Requests are free. You pay the instructor or centre directly for lessons.

Can I combine enrichment with tuition?

Yes. Mention it in either request and we will help you plan a weekly schedule that works.

My child's activity isn't listed.

Type it under "Something else" and send the request. We will let you know if we can help.
